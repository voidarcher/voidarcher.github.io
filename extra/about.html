About Me

Not really much to say. My main OS is Debian, which I landed on after the cliche Linux life cycle of Ubuntu, then Fedora, then Arch, then Slackware, then Gentoo, then deciding I hate having to do stuff like "uPdATe mY MaCHinE" and installing Debian 13 "Bullseye."

My hardware is completely unremarkable. A Dell Inspiron 15 5000 that I've had since 2020-ish. I actually bought a ThinkPad T14 Gen 3 back in 2024 with the intention of replacing the Dell but...I mean the Dell still works so here I am. Probably the same logic that caused me to write this website from scratch in nothing but HTML and CSS. Gonna make McMaster-Carr look like a dead turtle.
